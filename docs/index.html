About the Game

Welcome to the enchanted marketplace! You are a potion shop owner travelling through fairy-tale lands. Hire magical creatures, brew powerful potions, and charm every customer that walks through your door.

Each game lasts up to 15 rounds. Pick a Caravan, hire from the Fairy Godmother's shop, and battle snapshot opponents based on real players' builds. Max out your reputation to win — or lose it all trying.

Features

Wishing Star Spell Implemented

Transforms transformable hirelings into one of two upgraded forms chosen by the player.

Transformation System Implemented

Registry-based mapping of source hirelings to two possible outcomes. Extensible for future cards.

Card Type System Implemented

Hirelings, Assistants, and Spells with tiers, tribes, potion slots, and cast timers.

Upgrade System Designed

3 identical cards automatically merge into one stronger card (e.g. Little Piggy x3 = Big Bad Wolf).

Potion Stock & Potency Designed

Each potion has independent stock and potency values, upgradable via spells and assistants.

Reputation System Designed

Win by maxing reputation. The bar crunches in rounds 13-15 for dramatic finishes.

Caravans / Hero Powers Designed

Choose a travelling shopfront with a unique passive ability at the start of each level.

Champion Snapshots Designed

Pin round 15 builds to the weekly Champion Board. Challengers get 3 attempts to beat them.

Events & Weather Designed

Transition-round events (Loan Shark, GambleBees) and dynamic weather (Frog Fog, Spell-made Lightning).

Cosmetics Designed

Titles, profile characters with upgradeable animations, and 8 character options.

Foundation & Wishing Star Spell
  • Project initialized with TypeScript, Jest, and GitHub Pages.

  • Transformation registry with extensible registration for future cards.
  • Deep copy system ensures transformed cards don't share mutable state.
  • 17 unit tests covering all transformation paths and edge cases.
  • Created CLAUDE.md, README.md, FEATURES.md, and patch notes system.
  • GitHub Pages site with game info and patch notes viewer.
